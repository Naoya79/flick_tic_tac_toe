How to Play

Goal

Line up 3 of your colored pieces vertically, horizontally, or diagonally.

Actions

  1. Place: Put a piece from your hand onto an empty space.
    Cannot place adjacent to your own pieces!
  2. Flick: Select one of your pieces on the board and slide it.
    It slides until it hits a wall or another piece.
    If it hits a piece, that piece gets pushed (Chain Reaction).

Special Rules

  • You cannot repeat the board state from 2 turns ago (Ko rule).
  • If both players line up 3 at the same time, the one who moved LOSES.
